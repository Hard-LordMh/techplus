Quick Config Setup:
1. Sign up/Log in to Cloudinary (Free).
2. Copy your Cloud Name from the Dashboard.
3. Go to Settings (Gear icon) > Upload. Scroll to Upload presets, click Add upload preset, set the mode to Unsigned, and click Save. Use that preset name below.
